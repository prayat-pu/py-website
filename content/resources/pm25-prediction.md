Research on air pollution in Chiang Mai, specifically focusing on PM2.5 levels, which are a major contributor to health problems in the region.

### Project Highlights
- **Analysis:** Identified fire as a significant correlating factor influencing PM2.5 levels.
- **Prediction:** Developed a machine learning model to predict PM2.5 levels for the next 3 hours.
